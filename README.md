# AgroPulse-Sentinel (APS)

**A conceptual digital framework for cold-chain monitoring and livestock transport biosecurity.**

---

### 📌 Overview
AgroPulse-Sentinel is a project concept inspired by real-world challenges in veterinary inspection and food supply chain safety. Designed around practical observations of livestock transport, APS maps out how core temperature tracking, route transit delays, and tissue quality indicators can prevent spoilage and protect public health.

### 💡 Key Problem Solved
* **Cold-Chain Spoilage:** Monitoring consignment core temperatures to prevent meat and biological samples from spoiling in transit.
* **Logistics Delays:** Tracking route times to highlight delays that increase risk at inspection checkpoints.
* **Food Safety:** Creating a reliable, easy-to-use digital log for inspectors, drivers, and transport operators.

### ⚙️ Practical Risk Parameters
1. **Core Consignment Temperature:** Evaluates thermal stability to ensure cold-chain integrity.
2. **Tissue / Lymph Node Health:** Categorizes physical inspection markers for early detection of spoilage.
3. **Transit Duration:** Identifies unaccounted route delays that increase contamination risk.
